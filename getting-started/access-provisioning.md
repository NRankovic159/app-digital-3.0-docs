## Requesting access

To request access to the APP Digital Program systems (partner portal, staging environment, and this documentation hub):

1. Have your Partner Admin submit a request via the [Support](/support) section, specifying the systems and role needed for each person
2. The APP Digital Program team verifies the request against your organization's program agreement
3. Credentials and/or invitations are issued to each named user
4. New users complete account setup and are added to the relevant Partner UAT and project channels

{% hint style="warning" %}
Access requests typically take 2–3 business days to process. Submit requests early in the Due Diligence phase to avoid delaying Implementation.
{% endhint %}

## Roles & permissions

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Partner Admin | Full access: can request/revoke access for their organization, manage integration settings, approve go-live |
| Partner Editor | Can manage product content, configure CX settings, and respond to NPI/content requests |
| Partner Viewer | Read-only access to reports, documentation, and project status |
| APP Digital Program Team | Full visibility across all partner accounts for support, NPI operations, and readiness review |

## Deprovisioning

Access should be revoked whenever someone leaves the project or your organization:

* Partner Admins can deprovision their own org's users directly in the partner portal
* For urgent/security-related deprovisioning, contact [Support](/support) directly
* Access is also automatically reviewed and deprovisioned at program offboarding or contract end
