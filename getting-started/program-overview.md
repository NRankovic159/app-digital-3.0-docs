This post helps explain a bit more about the APP Digital Program structure as well as the structure of this documentation hub aimed to support you through each phase of the program.

## Program Phases

Our APP Digital Program follows a 5 phase timeline:

1. Pre-Sale (2 weeks)
2. Due Diligence (4-8 weeks)
3. Intro & Training (2-4 weeks)
4. Implementation (4-6 months)
5. Launch Preparation & Go-Live (2 weeks)

## Documentation Structure

This documentation hub is organized by topic rather than by program phase, so you can find what you need whether you're just getting started or already deep into implementation. Here's a quick overview of each section:

* **Getting Started** — You are here! Onboarding checklist, integration setup (Shopify API vs. Flat File), access/account provisioning, program glossary, and a first-project walkthrough.
* **CX Guidelines** — Design/UX standards, accessibility expectations, localization/RTL boundaries, and approved customization patterns.
* **NPI Operations** — Readiness checklists, content delivery SLAs, milestone explainers, and the missing-content-at-announce runbook.
* **Analytics** — How to read completeness/performance reports and what's measured and why.
* **Theme Release** — Version-by-version release notes, migration/upgrade guides, and deprecation notices.
* **Features & Functionality** — One landing page per capability area (Product Discovery, PDP, Cart & Checkout, and more), each with its own Change Log.
* **Support** — Partner UAT, Product Content Support (NPI), and Theme Support — priority definitions, SLAs, and filing process.
* **About Us** — The APP Digital program and its mission.

## How to use GitBook

* **Search** — use the search bar at the top of any page to find content across the entire hub; it indexes every published page automatically.
* **Left navigation** — each bold item in the left sidebar is a main section; click it to open that section's main page, and its sub-pages are listed underneath.
* **Change Logs** — every article under Features & Functionality has its own Change Log sub-page tracking what changed between versions.
* **Direct links** — every page has a stable URL, so you can bookmark or share a direct link to any article.
* **Requesting changes** — if you spot something missing or incorrect, use the support ticket and content-request links on the [Home](/home) page.
