## 1. Mandatory Merchandising Standards

All partner e-commerce sites must strictly follow Apple trademark and visual hierarchy rules:

- Never alter, crop, or skew official Apple product render angles.
- Always display trademark footer: *"iPhone, Apple Watch, and AppleCare are registered trademarks of Apple Inc."*
