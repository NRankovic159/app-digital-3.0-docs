# Wiki Governance & Hygiene

To keep this knowledge wiki valuable and prevent it from becoming outdated:

1. **Rule of Single DRI:** Every document has exactly **one primary owner** responsible for freshness.
2. **Review Cadence Alerts:** Any page whose `Last Verified` date is older than 90 days triggers a quarterly verification prompt.
3. **Draft vs Active Tagging:** Incomplete documentation must stay tagged `Status: Draft` with an estimated publication date.
4. **Deprecation Over Deletion:** Instead of deleting old runbooks, move them to an `Archive/` folder and tag `Status: Deprecated` with a pointer to the replacement page.
